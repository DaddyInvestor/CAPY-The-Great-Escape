# Mission 01 — แหกสวนสัตว์

Mission ID: CAPY-M01

Difficulty: Medium

Target duration: 6–10 minutes

## Story

A stubborn capybara wakes up inside the zoo and
decides to escape into the outside world.

## Mission Flow

1. Wake up inside the capybara enclosure.
2. Learn movement and interaction.
3. Escape the enclosure.
4. Avoid patrolling zookeepers.
5. Discover the maintenance area.
6. Choose an escape route.
7. Reach the outer gate.
8. Unlock Zone 02.

## Three Escape Routes

MAIN_GATE:
Find a keycard and open the main gate.

MAINTENANCE_TUNNEL:
Discover a hidden maintenance tunnel.

FENCE_ESCAPE:
Use crates and environmental obstacles.

## Mission 01A Scope

Only the static zoo environment is included.

Required:
- Zoo ground
- Perimeter
- Capybara enclosure
- Pond
- Paths
- Buildings
- Locked visual gates
- Spawn point
- Checkpoint markers

Out of scope:
AI, puzzle logic, mission completion, saving,
rewards, custom capybara character.

Checkpoint markers are visual only in M01A.
